The perception engine

The live piece the paintings borrow their motion from — a system perceiving a landscape and drawing what it sees. Reference, not product: it runs continuously and rations its own elements, so give it a minute before deciding nothing is happening.

Each file is several megabytes and loads everything at once — give it a moment on a slow connection. Published from a private repository; these are build outputs and editing them here has no effect.
